Private by design

Everything is worked out on your phone. PaiYo has no server, no account, no ads and no tracking.

  • Only bank and business alerts that mention an amount are read. Personal SMS are never stored and one-time passwords are never stored.
  • Your data is kept in an encrypted database whose key never leaves the phone; the text of each alert is deleted after 30 days.
  • Gmail (read-only), Drive backups and the Sheet are your own Google services, used only if you switch them on, with the narrowest permissions.
  • Backups are encrypted with a passphrase only you know before they reach your Drive. Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock.
