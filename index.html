Leakly

AIO Budgeting Solution for Android

Find where your money leaks. Leakly reads the bank, card and UPI alerts on your phone and turns them into a categorised spending log, automatically.

  • Budgets that start from your bills and usual spending, with alerts before you overspend
  • Bills and credit-card billing cycles, account balances, net worth and a dashboard
  • Excel export, your own Google Sheet, and backups to your own Google Drive

Private by design

Everything is worked out on your phone. Leakly has no server, no account, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
